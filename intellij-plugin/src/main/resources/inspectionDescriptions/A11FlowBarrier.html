Reports barriers and loop tails that cannot hold what they say.

until, while and a carry (x <- ...) belong to a repeat: this reports one written outside any repeat, a second stop condition, a carry naming something other than what the repeat carries, and a value carried twice.

It also reports orderings that cannot be honoured: an after naming a step written further down the flow, or naming the statement it is on; a second wait or drain on something already held for in the same body, which adds nothing because the first one already finished it; and a cancel of a call this body has already waited for, which has nothing left to stop.
